What data do we collect?

Softika collects the following data:

  • Personal identification information (Name, email address, phone number, etc.)

How do we collect your data?

You directly provide Softika with most of the data we collect. We collect data and process data when you:

  • Register online or place an order for any of our products or services.
  • Voluntarily complete a customer survey or provide feedback on any of our message boards or via email.

How will we use your data?

Softika collects your data so that we can:

  • Process your order and manage your account.
  • Email you with special offers on other products and services we think you might like.

How do we store your data?

Softika securely stores your data as email contact within our email account.

Softika will keep your personal information (name, email, phone number etc) until you request deletion. Once we receive such a request from you, your data will be removed by removing your email, name and any other personal information from our email contacts list.

What are your data protection rights?

Softika would like to make sure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights. Every user is entitled to the following:

The right to access – You have the right to request Softika for copies of your personal data. We may charge you a small fee for this service.

The right to rectification – You have the right to request that Softika correct any information you believe is inaccurate. You also have the right to request Softika to complete the information you believe is incomplete.

The right to erasure – You have the right to request that Softika erase your personal data, under certain conditions.

The right to restrict processing – You have the right to request that Softika restrict the processing of your personal data, under certain conditions.

The right to object to processing – You have the right to object to Softika's processing of your personal data, under certain conditions.

The right to data portability – You have the right to request that Softika transfer the data that we have collected to another organization, or directly to you, under certain conditions.

If you make a request, we have one month to respond to you. If you would like to exercise any of these rights, please contact us at our email office@softika.dev

Cookies

Cookies are text files placed on your computer to collect standard Internet log information and visitor behavior information. When you visit our websites, we may collect information from you automatically through cookies or similar technology.

For further information, visit allaboutcookies.org

What types of cookies do we use?

We want to assure our users that our website does not utilize cookies to collect or store personal information. We respect your privacy, and our commitment is to provide a straightforward and cookie-free browsing experience. We do not engage in any tracking activities that involve the use of cookies. Any information you choose to share with us, such as through contact forms or other direct input methods, is used solely for the purpose of fulfilling your requests, and we do not retain this data beyond what is necessary for that specific interaction. Our dedication to transparency and user privacy is fundamental to our online presence.

Changes to our privacy policy

Softika keeps its privacy policy under regular review and places any updates on this web page. This privacy policy was last updated on 15 January 2024.

How to contact us

If you have any questions about Softika's privacy policy, the data we hold on you, or you would like to exercise one of your data protection rights, please do not hesitate to contact us.

Email us at: office@softika.dev

How to contact the appropriate authority

Should you wish to report a complaint or if you feel that Softika has not addressed your concern in a satisfactory manner, you may contact the Information Commissioner's Office.
